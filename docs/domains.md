# Restaurant domain map (coverage checklist)

Every request, skill and metric is tagged with one domain. If a domain has no skills yet,
that is a known gap, not a blind spot.

## 1. Customer edge
**L3: demand and ordering platforms** (bring attention or orders)
- 3P marketplaces: DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub, ezCater, Catering marketplaces
- Virtual brands on each marketplace (one brick-and-mortar → several brands)
- Discovery: Google Business Profile, Maps, Yelp, Instagram, TikTok, Meta ads, creators
- First party: website, online ordering, app/PWA, QR, phone orders, catering page
Goal: maximize GMV per platform and brand, then shift customers to first party.

**L2: systems that process and hold data**
- POS (Toast, Clover, Square, Noe…), payments (Stripe, processors)
- Middleware (Orders.co, UrbanPiper, Otter, Chowly…)
- Loyalty, CRM, email/SMS, wallet passes, reviews/reputation
- Chargebacks, refunds, disputes, cancellations (money leaks)

## 2. In-unit operations
- Menu, recipes, pricing, availability (86s), hours
- Inventory and waste; prep and kitchen workflow
- Labor: scheduling, time tracking, tips, payroll inputs, hiring
- Equipment, maintenance, cleaning, food safety logs

## 3. Vendor and supplier
- Ordering and par levels, invoices, price changes, deliveries, substitutions, credits

## 4. Compliance, finance and admin
- Sales tax (CDTFA), permits, health inspections, licenses, insurance
- Bookkeeping, P&L, bank reconciliation, 3P payouts reconciliation
- Labor law, payroll deadlines, required postings
- Reminders and deadlines

## 5. Growth and expansion
- Catering and corporate accounts, events
- New virtual brands, new locations, franchising readiness (unit economics, SOPs)

## Metrics (every skill should move or measure one)
GMV · orders by channel · 3P→1P conversion · repeat rate · AOV · contribution margin ·
cancellations/chargebacks $ · catering leads and revenue · reviews and rating · hours saved
